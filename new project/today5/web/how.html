How LeadPulse works

LeadPulse is an AI sales assistant. It researches companies for you and every morning shows only the few worth contacting today, with the reason why now, the right person and a ready-to-send message.

📊 How the score works (0-100)

  • Fit (40): right industry (10) + based in India (5) + right company size (15) + how much they need what you sell (10, judged by AI).
  • Timing (40): fresh real signals in the last 90 days. Newer signals give more points.
  • Reachability (20): named person found (20), only a role (10), nothing (0).

Change your industries, company size and roles in Settings → Target customer.

⚡ Signal or noise?

Signals (count for timing): funding, new leader, hiring spike, new product, new location, big customer win, pricing change.

Noise (ignored): blog posts, design or text changes, footer, cookie banner, small edits.

✅ Can I trust the data?

  • Every fact has a source link. Facts without a real source are removed.
  • Source order: company website › recent news › directories › AI guess.
  • Confidence badge: high official or 2+ sources, medium one good source, low old or guessed.
  • When sources disagree you see a Conflicting badge with both values.

🗓 Today's top leads rule

  • At most 5 companies, highest score first.
  • Each must have a real signal in the last 30 days.
  • Done / Not relevant hides a company. Snooze hides it for 7 days.
  • If fewer than 5 qualify, fewer are shown. No weak fillers.

🧰 Everyday controls

Add: paste websites or upload a CSV (100+ is fine). You see a preview first.
⏸ Pause: pause one company, or Pause all to stop everything.
⬇ Export: one company or all, as CSV, Excel or PDF.
🗑 Delete: removes a company and all its data (asks first).
⏰ Automation: runs by itself every day at the time you choose (Settings → Automation).
🔌 Integrations: change API keys and backups anytime (Settings → Integrations).
